🧳 What to Pack

Essentials

  • Passport — must match booking name
  • SetSail Pass (printed or on phone)
  • Sunscreen — lots of it
  • Swimsuits
  • Casual resort wear
  • One nice dinner outfit
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sandals / flip flops

Good to Have

  • Light layers — ship A/C is cold
  • Motion sickness remedies
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Waterproof bag
  • Camera + memory cards
  • Portable charger
  • Kids' snacks + activities
  • Wedding day outfit 💍

Checked luggage may not reach your cabin until early evening — carry anything you need for the first afternoon.
